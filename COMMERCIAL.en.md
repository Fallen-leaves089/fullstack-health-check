[中文](COMMERCIAL.md) | **English**

This work is licensed under the **PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0** and is intended for noncommercial use by default.

If you want to use this work or a modified version for a commercial purpose, you need written permission from the author first. The author is open to discussion; you do not need a complete proposal in your first message.

## Cases worth discussing first

- Internal production use inside a company
- Embedding the review workflow in a paid product, SaaS, or hosted service
- Consulting, outsourcing, or client delivery projects
- Paid training, paid support, or certification courses
- Any other use that directly or indirectly produces commercial benefit

Personal learning, research, experiments, hobby projects, and qualifying noncommercial use by educational, charitable, public-research, or government institutions usually do not require a separate license. The `LICENSE` text controls if there is any conflict.

## How to start a conversation

Open an issue whose title starts with `[Commercial License]`, or email `chenshuidelaye@qq.com` with `[Commercial License]` in the subject. You may also contact the maintainer through the public channel where you found this package. In the first message, please describe:

- who will use it (individual, company, or organization)
- the intended use (internal tool, product feature, client delivery, training, and so on)
- expected scale (people, projects, deployments)
- whether you need modification, redistribution, white-labeling, or hosting
- the desired license term

The author can offer a one-time license, an annual license, a per-project license, or a custom support arrangement. The final terms are whatever the parties agree to in writing.

## Please do not

- Start commercial use first and ask for a license later.
- Treat "noncommercial" as "no direct fee". The definition in `LICENSE` and the actual use case control.
- Paste secrets, customer data, or unsanitized internal material into a public licensing request.
